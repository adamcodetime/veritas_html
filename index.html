About EquinoxAI

Curiosity about markets. Clarity in the experience.

EquinoxAI is an AI project exploring how historical market data can inform upcoming price estimates. We bring together a model trained on data from 1964–2017, ONNX inference, and a straightforward interface—so people can spend less time figuring out the tool and more time exploring it.
